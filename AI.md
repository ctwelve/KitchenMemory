# Artificial intelligence use

Kitchen Memory is developed with assistance from generative artificial
intelligence tools. This statement exists to make that assistance visible
without confusing a tool with an author or maintainer.

## How AI is used

AI tools may assist contributors with:

- exploring product and technical approaches;
- drafting and reviewing source code and tests;
- drafting and refining documentation;
- diagnosing build and implementation problems; and
- developing visual concepts and project assets.

The extent of that assistance varies by change. The Git history is the primary
record of how the project evolved; material AI assistance should also be noted
in commit or review context when doing so would help future contributors.

## Human responsibility

Human contributors direct the work, decide what belongs in the project, review
the resulting changes, and remain responsible for testing, correctness,
security, accessibility, licensing, and maintenance. AI output is treated as an
untrusted draft: it may be incomplete, incorrect, insecure, or derived from an
inappropriate assumption.

No AI system is identified as an author, contributor, maintainer, or copyright
holder of Kitchen Memory.

## Copyright and licensing

AI assistance does not change the project's license. Material committed to this
repository is accepted and distributed by its contributors under the GNU
General Public License, version 3 only (`GPL-3.0-only`), unless a component is
clearly identified otherwise. See [COPYRIGHT](COPYRIGHT) and [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Contributors are responsible for checking AI-assisted material and must not
knowingly submit content that infringes another party's rights or is
incompatible with the project's license.

## Privacy

Secrets, credentials, private household information, and users' recipe data
must not be supplied to an AI service merely to develop or troubleshoot Kitchen
Memory. Any future product feature that uses AI with user data will require a
separate, explicit design and privacy review.
