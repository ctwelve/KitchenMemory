## Product name

**Kitchen Memory**

The name describes the product beyond recipe storage. Source captures, maintained
recipes, planned cooks, pantry observations, cooking sessions, photographs,
deviations, and family knowledge are different things a kitchen can remember.

The name remains a working direction until availability and trademark research
are completed. Repository, bundle, and target renaming can wait until concrete
implementation begins.

## Primary tagline

> Cook, remember, improve.

This is the concise product promise. It reflects the central loop:

```text
Cook
  carry out a planned recipe

Remember
  retain what was intended, available, changed, and observed

Improve
  refine the recipe, pantry knowledge, planning, and future attempts
```

The line is short enough for an app listing, introductory screen, website header,
or About panel. Its tone is capable, humane, and iterative rather than nostalgic
or artificially intelligent.

## Secondary line

> The recipe is only the beginning.

This line expresses the larger product vision: a recipe becomes planning,
readiness, cooking, memory, collaboration, and accumulated household knowledge.
It has potential for launch copy, feature storytelling, documentation, or a
future campaign as the product grows into its full scope.

It should be retained even when the primary tagline is preferred. The two lines
serve different purposes:

- **Cook, remember, improve.** explains the recurring product loop.
- **The recipe is only the beginning.** opens the door to the broader vision.

## Voice

Kitchen Memory should sound:

- Warm without becoming sentimental by default.
- Capable without sounding like enterprise inventory software.
- Intelligent without treating “AI” as a personality.
- Honest about uncertainty.
- Comfortable with improvisation, failure, family humor, and imperfect kitchens.
- Respectful of source material and personal history.

The product may be playful, but controls and suggestions should remain plain and
trustworthy. Humor belongs in moments of discovery and personality, not in error
messages that obscure what happened.

## Supporting language

Additional lines worth retaining for possible use:

- Your kitchen remembers.
- Recipes, plans, and what really happened.
- A shared memory for your kitchen.
- What you planned. What you had. What happened.
- Recipes for real kitchens.

These are supporting copy, not competing taglines.
