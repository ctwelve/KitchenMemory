## Concrete corrections

1. **C9 full-digit million forms:** replace French `many` with `%1$(count)lld notes enregistrées` and Spanish with `%1$(count)lld notas guardadas`. `%lld` supplies digits, not the noun “million”; category `many` is not a directive to insert `de`. French written-digit versus spoken-million behavior is directly illustrated by [OQLF](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/25100/la-prononciation/prononciation-des-nombres/regles-generales-de-lecture-des-nombres); [RAE cardinal guidance](https://www.rae.es/dpd/cardinales) distinguishes numeral use from the noun millón. Likewise revise Portuguese to `%1$(count)lld notas salvas`, subject to Brazilian editorial confirmation. Keep the required category even when its wording equals `other`. [CLDR 48](https://www.unicode.org/cldr/charts/48/supplemental/language_plural_rules.html) category selection and actual numeric presentation are separate concerns. Do not conflate awkward zero wording with an incorrect CLDR category.

2. **C10 source and translations:** “Retry saves that same note” sounds like guaranteed success despite a retryable failure. Change source to “Retry tries to save the same note again,” then regenerate. Resolve whether submitted-note verbs imply network sending; Japanese `送信` is particularly worth reviewing. A candidate that describes the original save attempt can avoid asserting an upload. The draft already flags this but needs a disposition before import.

3. **L1 Korean:** adopt the proposed particle-independent rewrite, then review whether `초안` or a consistent app-wide draft term fits recovery copy. Arbitrary authored titles should not require guessing the final sound.

4. **C3/C12:** normalize Portuguese Session terminology as proposed; consider French `Facteur de quantité` instead of `Échelle` for recipe scaling. This latter change is an editorial candidate, not a certified correction.
